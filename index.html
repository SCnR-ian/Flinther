Simple, focused, built for table tennis

One tool for admins and coaches. No spreadsheets.

  • Court bookings — view and manage every court booking in a daily calendar; see what's booked and what's free at a glance.
  • Coaching schedules — schedule 1-on-1 and group coaching sessions; coaches log in to see their own timetable.
  • Social play sessions — run social and casual play sessions and let members join online.
  • Member management — add and manage members, track who's coaching with whom, and handle club admin in one place.

How it works: create your account, set up your club (name, courts and opening hours), then go live at yourclub.flinther.com and start taking bookings.
